## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-13

The first release: prayer-time calculation written from published astronomy and each method's
published definition, with no dependencies.

### Added

- `PrayerTimes`, which computes Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, sunset, Maghrib, Isha, and the
  night's middle and last third for one civil day in a time zone. Every event belongs to the
  day you asked for, even an Isha after midnight.
- `EventTime`, which reports an event that doesn't happen as `.doesNotOccur` with a
  `NonOccurrence` reason instead of inventing a time.
- `GregorianDay`, a validated civil date with an ISO 8601 `Codable` form.
- Fourteen `CalculationMethod` presets. A preset follows its authority's documented rules, or,
  for MUIS and the UAE, which publish only timetables, is fitted to those timetables.
  `METHODS.md` gives every value with its source.
- `CalculationParameters` with typed Fajr, Maghrib and Isha rules, Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr,
  method and user adjustments, rounding, and the night's span.
- High-latitude rules: `recommended`, `middleOfTheNight`, `seventhOfTheNight`,
  `twilightAngle` and `disabled`. Each prayer is bounded by its own night.
- The Moonsighting Committee's seasonal Fajr and Isha, with the night's seventh as a further
  bound from 55° of latitude.
- Opt-in country suggestions with sources, and an opt-in Ramadan rule for interval-based Isha.
- `Qibla.direction(from:)`.
- `docs/algorithm.md`, the normative specification, and RFC 0001 for the API.
- JSON vectors in `Vectors/`, generated by an independent reference implementation, for
  engines in any language.
- `docs/validation.md`, which reports parity with adhan-swift's test data, a year of official
  timetables, self-consistency, properties and a benchmark.
- A DocC catalog with articles on getting started, choosing a method, high latitudes and
  validation, published to https://salah-kit.github.io/SalahTimes/.
- `Examples/PrayerTimesCLI`, a command-line example that builds under both default-isolation
  settings.
- CI on macOS and Linux, with builds for iOS, watchOS, tvOS and visionOS.
