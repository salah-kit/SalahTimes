## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Compare official 2026 timetables from more places and authorities: Diyanet's Izmir and Van,
  JAKIM's zones PNG01, JHR02 and SBH07, Kemenag's Surabaya, Medan and Makassar, the University
  of Tehran's Institute of Geophysics, Egypt's General Authority for Survey and Jamia
  Uloom-ul-Islamia Banuri Town. `fetch.py` reads the Institute's PDFs with the standard
  library, and the comparison accepts a column an authority doesn't publish and a separate
  sunrise station.

- Document the complete 2025 Qatar Calendar House comparison, its OCR provenance and
  the retained 90-minute Isha policy, without adding a fitted preset adjustment.

- Import and compare complete official UAE public-page calendar exports without storing an
  API key or redistributing the source data.
- Add a Swift Package Index manifest linking to the published documentation.
- Document the release process in CONTRIBUTING, and point contributors to the open
  `help wanted` comparisons.

### Changed

- RFC 0001 cites SalahKit's public RFCs, and records how they answered its questions for
  SalahCalculation.

### Fixed

- Cite the authorities' own publications in METHODS.md where they exist (the Muslim World
  League's 1986 resolution, Diyanet's 2013 statement, the Fiqh Council of North America's
  current suggestion, Kuwait's official calendar, Banuri Town's fatwas), and correct what they
  contradict: Diyanet's 16° Isha covers its calendars for Europe and North America only, FCNA
  no longer uses the Moonsighting Committee's method, and several measured country differences.
- Compare JAKIM's zone WLY01 at the reference stations the Federal Territories Mufti publishes,
  not central Kuala Lumpur. Most of its offset from the `jakim` preset then goes, leaving
  upward rounding and a seasonal excess in Fajr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, and the zone's fit
  becomes rounding up with sunrise a minute earlier.
- Link CNN Indonesia's report of Kemenag's Subuh criterion at its current address, in
  METHODS.md and `Vectors/methods.json`; the old address returned 404.
- Document the independent JPL solar-event comparison and the 2026 UAE Asr difference,
  including a tested local adjustment. Existing method presets remain unchanged.
- Document the public date comparison and prayer-time decoder, and explain the CLI example's
  naming exception to the ecosystem standard.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-13

The first release: prayer-time calculation written from published astronomy and each method's
published definition, with no dependencies.

### Added

- `PrayerTimes`, which computes Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, sunset, Maghrib, Isha, and the
  night's middle and last third for one civil day in a time zone. Every event belongs to the
  day you asked for, even an Isha after midnight.
- `EventTime`, which reports an event that doesn't happen as `.doesNotOccur` with a
  `NonOccurrence` reason instead of inventing a time.
- `GregorianDay`, a validated civil date with an ISO 8601 `Codable` form.
- Fourteen `CalculationMethod` presets. A preset follows its authority's documented rules, or,
  for MUIS and the UAE, which publish only timetables, is fitted to those timetables.
  `METHODS.md` gives every value with its source.
- `CalculationParameters` with typed Fajr, Maghrib and Isha rules, Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr,
  method and user adjustments, rounding, and the night's span.
- High-latitude rules: `recommended`, `middleOfTheNight`, `seventhOfTheNight`,
  `twilightAngle` and `disabled`. Each prayer is bounded by its own night.
- The Moonsighting Committee's seasonal Fajr and Isha, with the night's seventh as a further
  bound from 55° of latitude.
- Opt-in country suggestions with sources, and an opt-in Ramadan rule for interval-based Isha.
- `Qibla.direction(from:)`.
- `docs/algorithm.md`, the normative specification, and RFC 0001 for the API.
- JSON vectors in `Vectors/`, generated by an independent reference implementation, for
  engines in any language.
- `docs/validation.md`, which reports parity with adhan-swift's test data, a year of official
  timetables, self-consistency, properties and a benchmark.
- A DocC catalog with articles on getting started, choosing a method, high latitudes and
  validation, published to https://salah-kit.github.io/SalahTimes/.
- `Examples/PrayerTimesCLI`, a command-line example that builds under both default-isolation
  settings.
- CI on macOS and Linux, with builds for iOS, watchOS, tvOS and visionOS.
